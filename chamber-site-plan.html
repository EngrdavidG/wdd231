Lagos Chamber of Commerce Site Plan

Site Name

Lagos Chamber of Commerce

This name represents businesses, entrepreneurs, investors, and organizations within Lagos, Nigeria. The website will serve as a hub for networking, business promotion, local events, and economic growth.

Site Purpose

The Lagos Chamber of Commerce website will provide information about local businesses, business events, membership opportunities, economic development, networking programs, and community activities.

The website will help connect business owners, entrepreneurs, investors, and residents while promoting local commerce and economic growth.

Target Market

Local business owners, entrepreneurs, investors, residents, startups, students, and visitors in Lagos and surrounding communities.

Site Goals

  • Promote local businesses and entrepreneurship.
  • Encourage networking opportunities among businesses.
  • Provide information about upcoming business events.
  • Support economic growth and development.
  • Increase membership engagement in the chamber.

User Personas

Small Business Owner

Ada is a fashion entrepreneur in Lagos who wants more visibility for her business and opportunities to connect with other business owners.

Corporate Investor

Michael is looking for investment opportunities, economic updates, and business partnerships within Lagos.

New Resident

James recently moved to Lagos and wants to discover trusted local businesses, events, and networking groups.

Scenarios

  • A business owner wants to learn how to join the chamber and explore membership benefits.
  • A visitor wants to check upcoming business workshops and networking events.
  • An entrepreneur wants to find local businesses and partnership opportunities.
  • A resident wants to support local businesses and explore community programs.

SEO Plan

  • Use keywords such as: Lagos businesses, chamber of commerce Lagos, Lagos entrepreneurs, business networking Nigeria.
  • Optimize meta descriptions and headings.
  • Use responsive web design for mobile devices.
  • Add Google Analytics integration.
  • Encourage backlinks from local businesses.

Design Brief

Primary Color: #1B1B3A (Dark Blue)

Secondary Color: #4CAF50 (Green)

Background Color: #F4F4F4 (Light Gray)

Text Color: #222222 (Dark Gray)

Font Family: Poppins
