Website Project Site Plan: EduPulse

Site Name

Name: EduPulse Portal

Reason for selection: The name blends "Education" with "Pulse," symbolizing a living, breathing system that keeps track of the real-time daily rhythm of a school. It shifts the perception of a school management platform from a static spreadsheet to a dynamic, vital hub for human connection.

Optional Domain Availability: edupulseportal.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of the EduPulse website is to serve as an empathetic, public-facing informational hub and portal demonstration for an all-in-one Nursery, Primary, and Secondary school management system. It showcases how administrators, teachers, and parents can easily access student daily tracking, classroom metrics, and administrative communications without technical friction.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1 (Parent Perspective): "I am a working mother with a toddler in nursery and a son in secondary school. How can I quickly check if my toddler napped today and verify when my older son's math assignment is due without logging into two different systems?"
  • Scenario 2 (Teacher Perspective): "As a primary school teacher managing a large classroom, can I quickly record attendance and update behavioral milestones on my tablet while moving around the room?"

Color Scheme

The colors below are defined in the external stylesheet and applied directly to this site plan to evoke a professional yet warm environment:

  • Primary Blue (#1e40af): Used for major headers, navigation elements, and branding to establish security, trust, and structural stability.
  • Soft Blue Background (#eff6ff): Used for content canvas shading and site backgrounds to keep the layout feeling light and glare-free for tired parents/teachers.
  • Warm Amber Accent (#f59e0b): Used for call-to-action buttons, notifications, alerts, and vital emphasis points to inject warmth and child-friendly approachability.

Typography

  • Heading Font: Fredoka - Used strictly for main titles (H1, H2, H3). Its rounded, soft edges project a friendly, child-centric, and humanized atmosphere.
  • Body Font: Inter - Used for all paragraphs, lists, forms, and data tables. It is a highly legible, geometrically stable sans-serif font engineered for excellent readability across mobile and desktop displays.
