Lagos Chamber Directory Site Plan

Site Purpose

The Lagos Chamber Directory website serves as a business resource hub where local companies can be listed and discovered by visitors, entrepreneurs, investors, and members of the community.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find businesses registered with the Lagos Chamber?
  • Which companies are premium chamber members?
  • How do I contact a business listed in the directory?

Color Schema

Primary: #004080

Secondary: #f4f4f4

Accent: #ffb703

Typography
